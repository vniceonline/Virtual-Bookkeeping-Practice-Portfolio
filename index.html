01 — About

I am a Philippines-based accountant with over 20 years of corporate accounting experience, currently expanding into virtual bookkeeping. I am developing hands-on proficiency in QuickBooks Online (QBO) and leveraging AI tools such as ChatGPT and Claude to support research, streamline communication, and enhance productivity.

I support small and medium-sized businesses with essential bookkeeping functions, including organizing financial documents, reviewing and identifying transactions, accurately categorizing income and expenses, and managing accounts receivable (AR) and accounts payable (AP) to maintain clean, audit-ready financial records.

I am committed to delivering reliable, detail-oriented, and high-quality bookkeeping support. With every engagement, my goal is to provide accurate and timely service that gives clients confidence in the integrity of their financial records.
